01 Research

Evidence built for the realities of work.

My research sits at the intersection of biomechanics, ergonomics, and occupational health. I study how physical work affects the upper extremity, then translate those findings into assessment methods that researchers and practitioners can use.

01

Overhead work

Developing exposure models and practical tools to predict acceptable duty cycles for above-shoulder tasks.

02

Industrial ergonomics

Translating biomechanics evidence into usable assessment methods for automotive manufacturing and proactive design.

03

Assistive technologies

Quantifying how passive upper-limb exoskeletons support workers across postures, task heights, and work-rest cycles.

04

Applied statistics

Using mixed-effects models, reproducible analysis, and clear visualization to strengthen ergonomics research.
